About Me

Professional DJ with a passion for atmosphere.

Kgalalelo Monageng, known professionally as DJ ASK, is based in Ga-Rankuwa Unit 16, Pretoria. He began his musical journey in primary school and officially launched his DJ career in 2015. Guided by mentors including DJ Tzee, Motion, and Mase, he has grown into a versatile event DJ, stage manager, and event coordinator with a strong reputation for keeping crowds engaged.

Whether it is a club night, private function, wedding, or corporate celebration, DJ ASK brings a polished sound, clear crowd reading, and event professionalism to every performance.
